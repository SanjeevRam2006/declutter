      What this is

      A simple talking space: you write, it replies plainly, you reply back. No scores, no streaks, no productivity system.

      How replies work: messages go through this local server to DeepInfra. The API key stays on the server. A basic responder is used when DeepInfra cannot reply.

      Your data: local storage is not encrypted. Clearing browser site data may remove chats. Deleting a chat removes it here, but cannot erase messages already sent to DeepInfra.

      Scope: this is a thinking companion, not a therapist. It helps you get your head straight and decide what to do next — it isn't a substitute for professional support.

      If you’re in distress, please reach out to someone you trust or a local support line. This app isn’t a substitute for professional help.
